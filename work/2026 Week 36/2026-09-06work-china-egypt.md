# CHINA STRENGTHENS TIES WITH EGYPT TO EXPAND ENERGY SIDE-CHANNEL INGRESS, U.S. FACING DEPLETING MUNITIONS

## MAJOR DEVELOPMENTS
* **Economy**: Chinese President **Xi Jinping**, Egyptian President **Abdel Fattah el-Sissi** met in Cairo. Jinping presenting China as **economic stabilizer** to contrast against the U.S., notably its long-running military involvement in the region. Egypt seeks to diversify its strategic partnerships.

* **Economy**: Jinping's visit followed his attendance of two-day **conference with Shanghai Cooperation Organization** in Kyrgyzstan's capital, **Bishkek**. Leaders of Russia, India, Pakistan, and Iran attended, sanctions aim to isolate Islamic Republic from remaining economic partners. 

* **Economy**: China has **invested >= $10bn** in Egypt. Government figures{*} estimate $20bn in annual trade between both countries. Annual trade between U.S. and Egypt was $15.7bn in 2025. In wake of the present America-Iran conflict, **Suez Canal**, Egypt controlled, becomes important alternative path to the **Strait of Hormuz**.

* **Military**: China and Egypt carried out a **multi-day air exercise** at several Egyptian air bases: the first combat exercise for China's **J-16 fighter jets**, Egypt's **Rafale fighter jets**.

* Xi Jinping makes **first trip to Egypt** (Grand Egyptian Museum) in a decade, first time in the region since Saudi Arabia visit in 2022. El-Sissi has made made several visits to China.
