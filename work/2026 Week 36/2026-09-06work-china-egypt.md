# MAJOR DEVELOPMENT CHINA STRENGTHENS TIES WITH EGYPT TO EXPAND ENERGY SIDE-CHANNEL INGRESS
## MAJOR DEVELOPMENTS
* **Chinese President Xi Jinping** and **Egyptian President Abdel Fattah el-Sissi** met in Cairo. Jinping presenting China as economic stabilizer to contrast with the U.S. and its long-running military involvement in the region.
* Xi Jinping made his trip to Egypt (Grand Egyptian Museum, near pyramids of Giza) in a decade, his first time in the region since Saudi Arabia visit in 2022.
